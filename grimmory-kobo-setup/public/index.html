Connect your Kobo

Plug your Kobo into this computer and this page sets it up to get books from the library by itself, using the library's Kobo sync: it connects the Kobo to your account. It makes a backup on the Kobo first, so every change can be undone.

Sign in first

Open the library, sign in, then come back to this page.

This browser can't reach your Kobo

Setting up from this page needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Open this page in one of those, or use the double-click file below instead.

1 Plug in your Kobo

  1. Connect the Kobo to this computer with its USB cable.
  2. On the Kobo, tap Connect.
  3. Click the button below and choose the drive called KOBOeReader. Chrome then asks whether this page may change files there: choose Allow (it says "Edit files" or "View and edit").

2 Choose what to set up

3 Setting up

    All done. Now:

    1. Eject the Kobo before unplugging it (Mac: the eject arrow next to KOBOeReader in Finder. Windows: "Safely Remove Hardware" in the taskbar, or right-click the drive and choose Eject).
    2. On the Kobo, connect to Wi-Fi and tap Sync (the circle arrows at the top of the home screen). Books you put on your Kobo shelf in the library arrive on the Kobo.

    Backups and undo

    Every time something here changes your Kobo, the old files are kept on the Kobo first. Undo puts them back. You can also save a copy of the Kobo's settings, reading progress and add-on folder to this computer.

    Not on Chrome or Edge? Use a double-click file

    These do the main setup (connect to your account, tidy up) from a file you double-click with the Kobo plugged in. They ask for your library username and password.

    • Mac: Connect my Kobo Open the download, then double-click Connect my Kobo. The first time, the Mac says it can't check the file: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
    • Windows: Connect my Kobo If the browser asks whether to keep it, choose Keep. Then double-click it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
